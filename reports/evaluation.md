Gold standard: **100 hand-labeled notes** (8 marked unsure). Labels follow [docs/labeling_guide.md](../docs/labeling_guide.md).

## How the gold standard was made

| Label source | Notes |
|---|---:|
| Labeled by the author, discussing notes with an AI assistant (Claude) | 38 |
| Drafted by Claude, reviewed and corrected by the author | 62 |
| Total | 100 |

The author changed **1 of 62** Claude drafts (2%) during review.

Because Claude helped produce the labels, these results measure agreement with an AI-assisted, human-reviewed reference, not with independent expert annotation. A second, independent human labeler would make the reference stronger.

## Limitations

- One person labeled the gold standard, without clinical training, with help from an AI assistant (see above). There is no second, independent labeler to measure agreement.
- 100 notes is a small sample; the confidence intervals show how uncertain each number is.
- Prompt versions after v1 were written after reading v1's errors on these same notes. There is no held-out set, so their scores are likely somewhat optimistic.
- MTSamples notes are transcription examples, not records from a real hospital.
